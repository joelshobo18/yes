# @theart0fwar format — breakdown

Studied on "The Story Of BloodHound Q50" (1.2M plays) and "The Story Of Amjad Ali" (240k), frame by frame at 1 fps.

* **Shape**: 60–73s "The Story Of X", one a day, voice-over only (deep calm narrator), no face, no music bed to speak of.
  Script ≈ 170–185 words: hook line → who they were → what they did → how it ended → sentence / death.
* **Visuals**: mostly stills (mugshot, press photo, social photo, evidence photo) held 3–8s with a slow push-in; short
  CCTV / news clips where they exist, with a red circle on the subject. ~12 visual changes per minute, each starting
  on the word that introduces the beat. Near-square photos sit over a blurred, darkened copy of themselves.
* **Captions**: 2–3 word chunks, caps, The Bold Font, centred just below the middle (~54%), white with yellow on key
  nouns/numbers and green on names, black outline + shadow, landing on the word. No titles, emoji or pop-ups.
* **Grade**: slightly desaturated, mild vignette.

`build_amjad_ali.py` = the rewritten Amjad Ali story: every fact checked against Slough Observer / Slough Express /
rdg.today coverage (sentenced 3 Sep 2026 at Reading Crown Court, £1,185,420 + £42,290 seized, warrants 12 Dec 2025).
Assets: Thames Valley Police release photos (watermark strip trimmed), Reading Crown Court (Wikimedia Commons),
three generated generic scenes (sorting centre, storage corridor, suitcase of cash). VO: ElevenLabs "Nathaniel C",
sped 1.08x to land in their length; words timed with faster-whisper.
